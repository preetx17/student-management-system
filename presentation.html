The Problem

Why Upgrade?

Legacy systems are holding institutions back.

Limitations of the Past

  • Data Loss: Paper registers and Excel files get corrupted.
  • Device Locked: Stuck on a single office PC.
  • No Security: Unrestricted access to private records.
  • Slow Workflow: Manual searches take minutes, not milliseconds.

The Modern Solution

  • Cloud Sync: Instant real-time updates everywhere.
  • Role Security: strict Owner vs. Teacher access.
  • Automation: 1-Click CSV bulk importing and exporting.
  • Universal UX: Flawless on smartphones and desktops.

Security

Bulletproof Defense

Enterprise-grade protection.

Cryptography

Bcrypt hashing (10 salt rounds) ensures passwords are mathematically impossible to reverse-engineer.

Network Guards

Strict IP rate limiting and HttpOnly session cookies block brute-force and XSS attacks.

Quality Assurance

Verification

Ensuring flawless execution.

Testing Strategy

  • Automated: Node.js scripts validate REST endpoints and DB state.
  • Boundary: Guaranteed rejection of invalid ages (<16 or >25).
  • Uniqueness: 409 Conflict handled gracefully for duplicate emails.
  • Sanitization: Malicious SQL payloads neutralized instantly.
